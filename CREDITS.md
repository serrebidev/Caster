# Credits

Caster exists because casting TO devices is easy and casting FROM your PC is mouse-first and half-broken. Send a file, a URL, your screen, one app window, or just this PC's sound to whatever is on your network.

Third-party projects it uses:

- ffmpeg (ships beside the executable; also needs to be on PATH for some targets).
- Chromecast, AirPlay (RAOP), UPnP/DLNA, Sonos, Roku, and Kodi are upstream casting protocols and devices, not code from this project.

Questions and bug reports are welcome in the [SerrebiProjects Telegram group](https://t.me/SerrebiProjects). More serrebi projects: [projects.serrebiradio.com](https://projects.serrebiradio.com).
